Our story

Started with one needle and a stubborn idea.

Creative Niddle began as a one-table workshop with a simple complaint: too many clothes look good on a hanger and wrong on a person. Everything we've built since has been an answer to that.

Why "Niddle"

It's a nod to the needle — the one tool that hasn't changed in a thousand years of clothes-making, even as everything around it has. We kept the misspelling because it started as a shorthand between two of our first tailors, and it stuck.

Every piece we make still passes through a real needle at some point, even the parts a machine handles first.

What "Everywear" means

We draft every block across a full size range from day one — never a single sample size graded up or down after the fact. Everywear means the same care goes into every size on the rack, not just the one on the model.

A shirt that only fits one kind of body isn't a design. It's an accident.

What we hold to

  • Fabric is chosen before the silhouette, never after.
  • Every size on the rack gets the same fitting attention, not a scaled-up guess.
  • Seams are finished the way we'd want them finished if we were paying for the shirt ourselves.
  • If a piece doesn't hold up to a season of real wear, we don't sell it.
